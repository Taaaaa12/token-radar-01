How the radar scores a protocol

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × launch × token-share × chains. Every input comes from the snapshot itself: no protocol name is ever special-cased.

  1. Category multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the tokenized protocols in the same category (at least 5 priced protocols, else the snapshot-wide median of —). Dexs price near —× TVL; liquid staking near —×.
  2. Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%..+120%, at half weight: 1 + growth × 0.5.
  3. Launch. By age with no token: under 90 days 0.70, 90–730 days 1.00 (the prime window), 730–1460 days 0.80, over 1460 days 0.55, unknown age 0.80.
  4. Token-share. 0.70 + 0.60 × the share of the category's protocols that already carry a token: categories where launching is the norm score higher.
  5. Chains. 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, up to 1.10.

What the radar leaves out, and why

Centralised exchanges never make the board (their token, if one ever comes, is a corporate decision, not a farmable launch), and versioned pools such as a protocol's V3 are pools of another protocol, not a launch of their own. Protocols listed with no token for over four years score low on launch likelihood — they have had their window.
